Tamba White Water Rafting

Our mission is to provide safe, exciting, and unforgettable white water rafting experiences. We bring people together through adventure while respecting and protecting our beautiful rivers.

History

Tamba White Water Rafting was created from a love of rivers, nature, and adventure. Our goal is to give every guest an opportunity to experience the excitement of rafting while enjoying the beauty of the outdoors.

We believe that safety, teamwork, and excellent customer service make every rafting trip memorable. We welcome beginners and experienced adventurers alike.
