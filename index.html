Liberia

Liberia is a country located on the west coast of Africa. It is known for its beautiful tropical rainforests, Atlantic Ocean beaches, rich culture, and friendly people. The capital city is Monrovia, and English is the official language. Liberia was founded by formerly enslaved African Americans in the 19th century and is Africa's oldest republic.
